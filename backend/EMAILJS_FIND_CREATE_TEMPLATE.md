# 🔍 How to Find "Create New Template" in EmailJS

## 📍 Where to Find It

### Step 1: Navigate to Templates Section

1. **Login to EmailJS**: https://www.emailjs.com/
2. **Look at the left sidebar menu** (or top navigation)
3. **Click on "Email Templates"** or **"Templates"**

### Step 2: Find the Create Button

Once you're in the Templates section, look for:

**Option A: Top Right Corner**
- Look for a **"+" button** or **"Add" button** or **"New Template"** button
- Usually in the top right corner of the page
- May say "Create Template" or "New Template"

**Option B: Empty State**
- If you have no templates yet, you'll see:
  - "Create your first template" message
  - A big **"Create Template"** or **"Get Started"** button
  - Click that button

**Option C: Template List**
- If you see a list of templates:
  - Look for a **"+" icon** or **"Add New"** button
  - Usually at the top of the template list
  - Or a floating action button (FAB) with a "+" icon

## 🔄 Alternative Paths

### If You Don't See "Templates" in Sidebar:

1. **Check Top Navigation Bar**
   - Look for "Templates" in the top menu
   - May be under "Email" or "Services" dropdown

2. **Check Account Menu**
   - Click your profile/account icon (top right)
   - Look for "Templates" or "Email Templates"

3. **Direct URL**
   - Try going directly to: https://dashboard.emailjs.com/admin/template
   - This should take you to the templates page

---

## 📱 Mobile/Responsive View

If you're on mobile or the interface looks different:

1. **Look for a hamburger menu** (☰) in the top left
2. **Click it** to open the sidebar
3. **Find "Email Templates"** in the menu
4. **Look for "+" or "Add" button**

---

## 🆘 Still Can't Find It?

### Check Your Account Type:
- **Free accounts** have full access to templates
- Make sure you're logged in to the correct account

### Try These:
1. **Refresh the page** (F5 or Ctrl+R)
2. **Log out and log back in**
3. **Check if you're in the right dashboard** (not a sub-page)
4. **Look for any "New" or "Create" buttons anywhere on the page**

---

## ✅ Once You Find It

After clicking "Create New Template" or "New Template":

1. You'll see a template editor
2. **Template Name:** Enter `Complaint Verification`
3. **Subject:** Enter `{{subject}}`
4. **Content:** Enter `{{message}}`
5. **Save** the template
6. **Copy the Template ID** (starts with `template_`)
